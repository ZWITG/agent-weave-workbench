## Family relationships, interactions, and relationship bars

### Takeaway
Each family member (parents, siblings, step-relatives, nieces and nephews) has a relationship bar. Low bars make positive interactions (conversation, compliment, gift) less effective and make the NPC more likely to insult, argue with or attack you. Hostile choices (insult, assault, repeatedly asking for money) push the bar down further.

### Cited Findings
- The relationship options you see depend on the relationship level. Family members with a low bar respond less well to conversations, compliments and gifts, and are more likely to insult, argue with or attack the player. — [BitLife Wiki: Relationships](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships)
- In an argument the character can apologize, agree to disagree or complain to their mother, insult the other person, or assault them. Insulting or assaulting lowers the relationship. — [BitLife Wiki: Relationships](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships)
- "Ask for Money" is a parent interaction in the Relationships tab. Parents tend to be more generous when the relationship is high. — [Playbite](https://www.playbite.com/q/how-to-ask-your-parents-for-money-in-bitlife)
- The "Mooch" ribbon is given to someone who keeps asking family for money even after being refused. — [BitLife Wiki: Relationships](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships)
- One way to reach a 0% relationship with a parent (needed for the Problem Child challenge) is to insult them and keep asking for money. — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-complete-problem-child-challenge-parent-juvie-expelled-school/)
- Siblings with high petulance can insult or assault the player in a childhood event where the player refuses a pet. Nieces and nephews are the children of the main character's siblings. — [BitLife Wiki: Childhood events](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Events/Childhood_events)
- Version 1.24 added stepfamilies (step-parents and step-siblings). — [Level Winner: Stepfamily Update 1.24](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-stepfamily-update-guide-version-1-24-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-stepfamily-update/)
- The most reliable way to raise a relationship is to spend time with the person. Visiting family also raises happiness and can lead to perks such as an inheritance from parents. — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/bitlife-improve-relationships-how/)
- Player advice from 2018: doing at least one interaction per person each year gets bars to 100% easily. "Talk with them" and "spend time" are the safe options. — [AppleVis comments](https://applevis.com/comment/93348)
- Since version 1.38 (June 2020 Pride update) there is a random chance of being born to two dads or two moms. — [Level Winner: Pride Update 1.38](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-pride-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-bitlife-version-1-38-aka-the-pride-update/)

### Inferences
- The core interaction loop has stayed the same since launch: positive interactions such as spend time, conversation, compliment and gift raise the bar, and hostile ones such as insult, argue and assault lower it. The exact list for each relative probably varies with age and relationship type. I could not confirm the full late-2026 list.

### Gaps
- I could not get a full, current list of interactions for each relative type (grandparents, aunts and uncles, cousins), because the wiki could not be fetched.
- I could not verify whether interactions changed after 2025.

## Love: dating, apps, hookups, proposals, weddings, prenups, divorce, affairs, arranged marriage, polygamy

### Takeaway
Romance runs through Activities > Love: dating, a dating app with age and net-worth filters, and hookups. After that it runs through the partner's profile: propose with a ring, plan the wedding, divorce. Prenups protect assets each partner had before the marriage. Arranged marriages happen in some countries. I found no source confirming polygamy, and one source says you can have only one lover at a time.

### Cited Findings
- To date: Activities > Love > Dating App. A date costs about $100, and you then raise the relationship rating. — [WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-love-and-relationships-guide-and-tips/) / [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-marry-someone-rich-in-bitlife/)
- The Dating App lets you filter by preferred age and net worth, which is useful for marrying rich. — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-marry-someone-rich-in-bitlife/)
- The Pride update (1.38) added a gay dating app. — [Level Winner: Pride Update](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-pride-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-bitlife-version-1-38-aka-the-pride-update/)
- Hookups (flings) are one-night partners. Afterwards they stay reachable as "Ex-Fling" in the Exes tab. You can have only one lover at a time, but you can cheat with a hookup and your lover can cheat on you. — [BitLife Wiki: Hook-Up](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Hook-Up)
- To propose: buy a ring at a jewelry shop, then use Propose on the partner's profile. A rejection usually means the relationship bar is too low. After acceptance, "Plan the wedding" offers venues in different price ranges. — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-marry-someone-rich-in-bitlife/)
- After about 3 years of engagement, the fiancé(e) demands the wedding. — [BitLife Wiki: Relationships/Spouses](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships/Spouses)
- A partner with a lot of money may demand a prenup. A pop-up shows both net worths. If you decline, they may leave you. Without a prenup, assets are split 50/50 on divorce. A prenup protects only pre-marriage assets; wealth earned during the marriage is still split. — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/what-does-a-prenup-do-in-bitlife-answered); [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-get-a-prenup/); [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/games/what-is-a-prenup-in-bitlife-and-what-do-they-do-215537/)
- To divorce, use the Divorce option on the spouse's profile. Older versions let you file at any time. You can ask an ex-spouse to get back together, and they sometimes accept. — [ProGameGuides: divorce](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-a-divorce-in-bitlife/)
- Affairs: you can leave your spouse and marry your lover if they are willing to get serious. Without a prenup and with a large wealth gap, a cheating spouse can walk away with a large payout. — [ProGameGuides: prenup](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/what-does-a-prenup-do-in-bitlife/); [Game Rant: Gold Digger](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-to-complete-gold-digger-challenge-divorce/)
- Arranged marriage happens when you are a native of certain countries. Sources disagree on which ones. One lists Afghanistan, India, Iran and Japan; another adds countries such as China, Nigeria and Saudi Arabia. Refusing almost certainly empties the bar with both parents. If you accept, you can divorce later. There is an "Arranged Marriage" achievement. — [KiwiPoints](https://www.kiwipoints.com/how-to-get-an-arranged-marriage-in-bitlife/); [Distractify](https://www.distractify.com/p/how-to-get-arranged-marriage-bitlife); [Playbite](https://www.playbite.com/q/how-to-arrange-marriage-bitlife)
- Players can marry into royalty in monarchy countries. — [Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/how-to-marry-royalty-bitlife/)
- The July 2026 official patch notes fixed a bug where a dead romantic partner kept appearing in relationships. — [BitLife Patch Notes (official)](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new/patch-notes/) (search summary only)

### Inferences
- "One lover at a time" plus affairs and hookups suggests BitLife supports cheating but not formal polygamous marriage. This is an inference only; I found no polygamy source.

### Gaps
- Polygamy: no source found either way.
- Dowry: no source found.
- I could not verify late-2026 dating-app costs or hookup mechanics (such as STD risk or pregnancy from hookups).

## Children: fertility, IVF, surrogacy, adoption, child-rearing, custody, generations

### Takeaway
From age 18, Activities > Fertility offers IVF, Surrogate, Donate Sperm and Vasectomy. Surrogacy is legal only in some countries, following real-world law, and costs about $25k to over $100k. Adoption requires being 21+ with a job and a home. When you die you can continue as an eligible child, including adopted children. Unlimited generations need Bitizenship.

### Cited Findings
- The Fertility tab is under Activities from age 18. It lists Donate Sperm, IVF, Surrogate and Vasectomy. — [ProGameGuides: surrogacy](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/where-is-surrogacy-legal-in-bitlife/)
- Surrogacy costs: the cheapest is about $25,000, and some surrogates cost over $100,000. Where surrogacy is legal follows real life, similar to how the game handles estate tax. — [ProGameGuides: surrogacy](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/where-is-surrogacy-legal-in-bitlife/)
- Both male and female characters can use IVF if they cannot conceive naturally. The partner has to agree, and arguments about it can happen. IVF and artificial insemination can produce twins or triplets. — [BitLife Wiki: Fertility](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Fertility); [Game Rant: twins/triplets](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-twins-triplets-how-ivf-artificial-insemination-fertility/)
- There is a "Fertile" ribbon for having many children. — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-get-fertile-ribbon-kids-fertility/)
- Surrogacy was added in the Pride update (1.38). One guide says surrogacy is unavailable to characters who have had a sex change. — [Level Winner: Pride Update](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-pride-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-bitlife-version-1-38-aka-the-pride-update/)
- Adoption is in Activities and requires being 21+ with a job and a residence. It shows a list of children with their health, smarts, looks and behavior. — [BitLife Wiki: Fertility](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Fertility) (search summary)
- Continuing as a child (added around version 1.14): on death you can play as an eligible child, adopted children included. Gamezebo advises having more than one child, because a child may die or be in prison. You can edit your will to leave everything to one child. — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-14-faq-how-to-continue-as-your-child-how-to-repeat-a-bitlife-and-how-to-become-a-model/)
- The Generations wiki page says the new generation's start reflects how the previous character died and what was inherited. Some countries charge estate tax. Bitizens get unlimited generations; non-Bitizens can continue only once. — [BitLife Wiki: Generations](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Generations)
- As of 2022, Startup Businesses could not be passed to heirs. A future update to allow it was mentioned but not confirmed. — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/can-children-inherit-startup-businesses-in-bitlife/); [Touch, Tap, Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/do-children-inherit-your-business-in-bitlife-answered/)

### Inferences
- Gating generations behind Bitizenship is a key monetization point to flag in any guide.

### Gaps
- Custody and child-support mechanics after divorce: none of my searches found a BitLife-specific source.
- Child-rearing interactions (for example, read a story, teach, discipline): I could not get a list.
- Whether businesses are inheritable as of late 2026 is unknown.

## Friends, enemies, coworkers, classmates; fame and social media

### Takeaway
Social media accounts grow followers. About 100k followers (sources vary, some say 25 fame is also needed) lets you apply for verification. At roughly 300k–350k followers you become a "Social Media Star", which unlocks the Fame tab. Fame also comes from careers such as acting, music and sports.

### Cited Findings
- Verification thresholds disagree between sources. One says 100k followers plus 25 fame. Another says 100,000 followers on one platform. A third suggests applying at around 250,000. — [ProGameGuides: Influencer Challenge](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-influencer-challenge-guide/); [ProGameGuides: Influencer](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-how-to-become-an-influencer/); [walkthroughs.net](https://walkthroughs.net/bitlife-guide-how-to-become-social-media-star-in-bitlife/)
- The Social Media Star status unlocks the Fame tab. One source puts it at 350,000 followers on a platform, another at about 300,000. — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-how-to-become-an-influencer/); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/bitlife/news/how-to-be-famous-in-bitlife)
- Fame can also come from acting, music, athletics and similar careers. — [Playbite](https://www.playbite.com/q/how-to-get-verified-on-social-media-in-bitlife); [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2024/12/29/bitlife-how-to-get-verified-in-social-media-platform/)
- The Influencer Challenge requires verified accounts on every platform, each with over 2 million followers. — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-complete-the-influencer-challenge-in-bitlife); also [Game Rant: verified](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-get-verified-social-media/)
- The September 2026 Movie Director Expansion Pack is described as the game's biggest update yet, and it is fame and career related. — [BitLife What's New (official)](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new) (search summary only)

### Inferences

## Pets, exotic pets, horses, zoo

### Takeaway
Ordinary pets come from shelters and breeders. Exotic pets, horses and llamas are for adults only. Exotic pets come from the Exotic Pet Dealer (one animal per year) or from rescuing animals in random encounters. Horses need an equestrian property, except a rescued unicorn. Some sources say non-premium players can own only traditional pets. The Zookeeper Expansion Pack (May/June 2024, v3.14.1) lets you run a small, medium or large zoo.

### Cited Findings
- Exotic pets, horses and llamas are adult-only (18+). — [BitLife Wiki: Exotic pet](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Exotic_pet)
- Non-premium accounts can own only traditional pets. MOD APK sites also list Exotic Pets as a Bitizenship perk, but I treat those sites as unreliable. — [Level Winner Pets Guide](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-pets-guide-tips-tricks-for-getting-pets/) (search summary)
- The Exotic Pet Dealer offers one animal per year. Rescuing an animal in a random encounter can let you keep it. — [BitLife Wiki: Exotic pet](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Exotic_pet)
- Horses need a property with "equestrian" in its name, costing about $5–15M. A rescued unicorn does not need one. — [WriterParty pets guide](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-complete-pets-guide-list-of-all-pets-and-how-to-get-and-use-them/); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-all-pets-in-bitlife/)
- Further pet lists are in: [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-life-simulator-a-list-of-the-new-pets-what-they-do-and-how-to-get-them/), [iFanzine](https://ifanzine.com/bitlife-pets-guide-all-pets-listed-and-how-to-get-them/), [BitLife Wiki: Pets](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Pets)
- The Zoo/Zookeeper Expansion Pack shipped with v3.14.1. The wiki says May 2024 for Android; ProGameGuides says 1 June 2024. Zoos come in small, medium and large. — [BitLife Wiki: Zoo](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Zoo); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/when-is-the-zookeeper-expansion-pack-update-coming-to-bitlife/)
- A siblings-and-pet childhood event exists, where you can refuse a sibling's request for a pet. — [BitLife Wiki: Childhood events](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Events/Childhood_events)

### Gaps
- I found no source naming a separate "Pet Pack".
- Pet death, vet visits and pet interactions are unverified in this session.

## Activities menu items

### Takeaway
The Activities tab collects most actions, and you can use it an unlimited number of times per year. The items I could confirm are: Adoption, Casino, Doctor, Emigrate, Fertility, Gym/Mind & Body (library, meditation, memory test), Horse racing betting, Identity, Lawsuit, Licenses (driver's, boating, pilot), Lottery, Love (dating app, hookup), Pets, Plastic Surgery, Rehab, Salon & Spa, Social Media, Vacation and Witch Doctor. Nightlife, Surrender, Shopping, Will, Crime and Sports are mentioned in guides, but I have no detail on them.

### Cited Findings
- Guide categories include Health, School/Work, Relationships, Crime, Mind & Body, Assets and Nightlife. The activities listed include dating, vacations, casino and horse race betting, lottery, plastic surgery, social media, rehab, pets, and salon and spa. You can do an unlimited number of activities every year. — [MrGuider](https://www.mrguider.org/cheats/bitlife-life-simulator-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/); [Playbite](https://www.playbite.com/q/what-is-every-activity-in-bitlife)

## Countries and locations: laws, monarchies, military draft, dowry, languages; events; packs and current state

### Takeaway
Country affects which systems apply:
- Royalty exists in about 17 monarchies.
- Arranged marriage happens in some countries.
- Surrogacy legality and estate tax follow real-world law.
- Execution is unavailable to rulers in some monarchies.

Official 2026 updates include the Movie Director Expansion Pack (September 2026), multi-language app support (31 August 2026), Ultimate Fighter Mode changes (July 2026) and Halloween challenges (October 2026). I found nothing on the military draft or dowry.
