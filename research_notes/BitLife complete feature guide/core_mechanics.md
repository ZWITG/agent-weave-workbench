## 1. Character creation (name, gender, country/city, custom lives, scenarios, premium options)

### Takeaway
A New Life can be random or customized from the "New Life" menu, which includes a "Custom People" builder. The paid God Mode pack opens editing of your own and NPC stats and attributes, and Bitizenship (a one-time purchase) adds unlimited generations, appearance changes and exclusive content. Current US prices are reported as God Mode $9.99, Bitizenship $7.99, and both bundled for $15.99. I found no reliable sourced detail on the "scenarios" options.

### Cited Findings
- Custom characters are made from the main menu: "New Life" leads to a "Custom People" button, where players build characters based on themselves, family, friends or fictional figures — [Ark Times blog (fan)](https://blog.wordpress.arktimes.com/?p=8452)
- God Mode is an in-app purchase that gives an editor for adjusting a character's life. The 1.33 update reportedly let players edit "just about anything", including their own stats and basic info and those of NPCs, with some limits — [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)
- One player reports that NPCs can be edited repeatedly during play, but your own new-game self can only be edited on the creation screen — [AppleVis forum](https://applevis.com/comment/110668)
- A player reports that Fertility is one of the stats settable in God Mode, and that it was visible only before a life starts (anecdotal) — [AppleVis forum](https://applevis.com/comment/110799)
- Pricing: one guide (about 18 days old as of 2026-10-25) says the US App Store lists God Mode at $9.99, Bitizenship at $7.99 and a bundle of both at $15.99, and that prices vary by platform and country — [ezechax.club (fan guide)](https://ezechax.club/bitlife-god-mode/). Mod-APK sites list conflicting lower prices (God Mode $4.99, Bitizenship $4.99, "Boss Mode" $2.99). These are unreliable and should not be used.
- Bitizenship is reported as a one-time IAP that includes unlimited generations, appearance changes, interaction with any teacher or celebrity, exclusive storyline content, more salon/spa options, pet store access, Dark Mode and no ads. "Royal status" and "unlimited job offers" are also mentioned — [ezechax.club](https://ezechax.club/bitlife-god-mode/); [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)
- Mod APK listings in 2026 reference versions v3.22–v3.25.3, which suggests the live game was at about v3.25 in late 2026. These sites are not trustworthy beyond the version number — [getmodsapk](https://getmodsapk.com/bitlife-apk-mod/); [bitlifemodapk.site](https://bitlifemodapk.site/)

### Inferences
- Editing your own character in God Mode appears limited to the creation screen, while NPC editing is ongoing during play. This rests on a single forum report.
- Because Bitizenship includes "unlimited generations", the free tier presumably limits how many generations you can continue. This is inferred from the Bitizenship feature list.

### Gaps
- Exact creation fields could not be verified from sources. [background, unverified]: first/last name, gender (male/female; later updates added more identity options), country and city, random-life button, and "Custom" life with chosen parents/family.
- "Scenarios" or challenge-start options on the New Life screen: no reliable source found.
- Official current pricing and the content of the God Mode and Bitizenship packs could not be confirmed from the App Store (blocked).

## 2. Core stats and hidden stats

### Takeaway
The four visible stats are Happiness, Health, Smarts and Looks, generally on a 0–100% scale. Hidden or attribute stats include Karma, Willpower, Discipline, Fertility and Craziness, and many of them are exposed or editable through God Mode. Sourcing on how hidden stats change is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Players set traits such as intelligence (Smarts), Looks, Health and Happiness to shape a character's success — [Ark Times blog](https://blog.wordpress.arktimes.com/?p=8452)
- Health determines lifespan and resistance to illness — [playbitlifegame.com (fan wiki)](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)
- Karma reportedly determines how a character reacts to challenges and how long they live, and rises or falls with good or bad deeds — [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)
- Willpower is tied to resisting negative actions such as heavy drinking or cheating. It arrived with the God Mode overhaul and can also be raised through positive deeds — [Prima Games](https://vip-develop.primagames.com/?p=64046); [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)
- Discipline measures underachiever vs. overachiever, and most stats range from 0 to 100 — [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)
- Craziness is a hidden attribute that can be edited for children and grandchildren, and siblings also have it — [AppleVis forum](https://www.applevis.com/comment/110817)
- Fertility is God Mode-settable — [AppleVis forum](https://applevis.com/comment/110799)
- In school, Grades start based on Smarts, while Popularity depends on several factors — [Levelwinner school update guide v1.23](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-school-update-guide-version-1-23-grades-and-popularity-cheating-on-tests-school-bullying-student-teacher-interactions-and-college-life-explained/)

### Inferences
- Karma links to the secret "Model Bitizen" ribbon (see section 6), which suggests Karma is tracked across the whole life and affects outcomes at death.

### Gaps
- No sourced detail on Athleticism or Sexuality. [background, unverified]: Athleticism affects sports/athlete careers; Sexuality is shown as a slider in God Mode; Looks decline in old age.
- [background, unverified] Common stat changers: Mind & Work activities (meditation, library, gym, walks) raise Happiness, Smarts and Health; plastic surgery, salon and spa raise Looks; drugs, alcohol, illness and assault lower Health.

## 3. Life stages, the Age +1 loop and event popups

### Takeaway
I found almost no sourced material on life stages. The game loop is turn-based: each "Age +1" tap advances one year and can trigger random scenario popups. At death, a tombstone summarizes the life and awards a ribbon.

### Cited Findings
- Repeatedly spamming the age button without doing anything is the route to the "Lazy" ribbon — [Levelwinner ribbons guide](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-ribbons-guide-how-to-get-all-the-ribbons/)
- The "surrender" option (suicide) is available at any age. Using End Life early is linked to the "Wasteful" ribbon — [Touch Tap Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-die-in-bitlife/); [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-ribbons-guide-how-to-get-all-the-ribbons/)
- At death, a tombstone shows total age, final net worth, achievements and a ribbon — [playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)

### Inferences
- The Lazy and Wasteful ribbons suggest the game scores how actively you play each year and when the life ends.

### Gaps
- Age gates could not be sourced. [background, unverified]: baby years 0–4 with limited actions; primary school from about 5–6; secondary/high school about 12–18; dating, part-time jobs and a driver's license in the teens; full-time jobs, university and most activities at 18; retirement and pension options at higher ages; elderly decline in Looks and Health.

## 4. Education

### Takeaway
School tracks Grades (based on Smarts) and Popularity. Cliques need high popularity and can reject you; joining a sports team helps. University majors gate graduate schools: law, medical, business, dental, pharmacy, nursing and vet. Fan guides disagree on the exact major-to-school mapping, and Smarts above about 80–85 is the common advice.

### Cited Findings
- Version 1.23 "School Update" added Grades and Popularity, test cheating, bullying and student-teacher interactions — [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-school-update-guide-version-1-23-grades-and-popularity-cheating-on-tests-school-bullying-student-teacher-interactions-and-college-life-explained/)
- Picking on classmates lowers Popularity, and friendly interactions raise it — [Levelwinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-school-update-guide-version-1-23-grades-and-popularity-cheating-on-tests-school-bullying-student-teacher-interactions-and-college-life-explained/)
- Cliques require a certain popularity and can reject you. The wiki lists cliques such as Nerds and Skaters, and the Mean Girls clique is also covered — [BitLife Wiki: Education/Cliques](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Education/Cliques)
- The Mean Girls clique reportedly needs about 80% Looks and a green class popularity bar. Joining a sports team makes high popularity easy — [Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-into-the-mean-girls-clique-in-bitlife/); [Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/bitlife-how-to-join-the-mean-girls-clique/)
- School options reportedly include local private schools, behavior schools and a private residential boarding school "far away". This comes from a low-quality site — [visionengicons (low quality)](https://visionengicons.com/review/cc9e44-how-to-get-into-private-school-bitlife)
- College funding options are parents paying, a student loan, or a scholarship — [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=22687)
- Major-to-graduate-school mapping in one guide: Chemistry leads to dental, medical, pharmacy and vet; Nursing leads to dental, medical and nursing; Political Science and English lead to law; Psychology leads to medical — [Progameguides schools guide](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-schools-guide/). Conflicts: one guide says law school requires Criminal Justice, and another says dentistry needs Biology — [Progameguides law](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-go-to-law-school-in-bitlife/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-law-school-medical-school-any-school/)
- Guides advise starting Smarts at about 80% or higher, Smarts above 85 and Health above 80 for doctors. Law school is called the hardest to qualify for — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-law-school-medical-school-any-school/); [Prima Games medical](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-get-into-medical-school-in-bitlife)
- Graduate-school career outcomes: business school leads to corporate jobs and possibly CEO; law school leads to lawyer, then judge or magistrate; pharmacy pays well; vet school is for working with animals; dental school leads to dentist — [AppGamer school guide](https://www.appgamer.com/bitlife/strategy-guide/school-guide); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-law-school-medical-school-any-school/)
- The "Academic" ribbon requires completing postgraduate education such as graduate or medical school — [Prima Games ribbons](https://primagames.com/tips/bitlife-ribbons-list-how-to-get-all-ribbons-in-bitlife)

### Inferences
- The conflicting major mappings probably reflect changes across versions. The report should present the Progameguides mapping as "commonly reported" and recommend checking in-game.

### Gaps
- No source found on community college or dropping out. [background, unverified]: community college is an option when rejected from university; you can drop out of high school or university; you can retake a GED later.
- Scholarship award mechanics (Smarts thresholds), student-loan repayment, and school clubs/activities (sports, band, drama) are not sourced.

## 5. Health, doctors, addictions, death and generations

### Takeaway
Illnesses and STDs are treated mainly through the Doctor in the Activities tab. A witch doctor (accessible when homeless) is risky and can kill you. Addictions can be cured at rehab. Death causes include old age, untreated illness (e.g. COVID, malaria, lupus), accidents, murder, assault and surrender. After death you can start fresh or continue as a child, inheriting wealth through a will. Bitizenship unlocks unlimited generations.

### Cited Findings
- Death routes include surrender (suicide, any age), old age, witch-doctor treatments (death risk rises with visits), failed murder attempts, assault, and illnesses. Untreated coronavirus kills quickly, but treated it is unlikely to be fatal. Lupus is likely fatal after about a year, and malaria very likely fatal within a year — [Touch Tap Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-die-in-bitlife/)
- Other death causes include accidents, illness, crime and substance abuse. Peacetime military service can bring fatal accidents, injuries or illness — [playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/); [thegunzone](https://thegunzone.com/?p=994420)
- STDs come from unprotected sex while clubbing or from Love > Hook Up, and are cured by visiting the Doctor in Activities — [Progameguides achievements](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-achievements-guide/)
- The witch doctor is reportedly available only when living on the streets, requires an existing disease, and may kill you. This detail comes from a Doglife guide, but the Witchcraft achievement also exists in BitLife — [Gamezebo Doglife](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/doglife-bitlife-dogs-achievement-guide-how-to-unlock-every-achievement-in-the-game/)
- The wiki maintains a Diseases page, which could not be fetched — [BitLife Wiki: Diseases](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Diseases)
- Health-related achievements: Addicted (three addictions at once), Bubonic Plague, Foam at the Mouth (rabies), Sickly (10 diseases in one life), Successful Rehab (cure an addiction at rehab), Witchcraft (witch doctor), and "It's a Miracle" (a cult cleansing a member of disease) — [Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-achievements-guide/); [TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/guides/bitlife-achievements-list-how-to-do-them/)
- Plastic surgery: the "Barbie Girl" ribbon requires multiple plastic surgeries as a female character. Botox injections are the "Pride" task in the Seven Deadly Sins scavenger hunt — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/bitlife-ribbons-list-how-to-get-all-ribbons-in-bitlife); [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=562809)
- At death you can start a new character (even in another country) or continue the legacy as one of your children, preserving family wealth — [playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)
- A will and testament in Activities lets you leave money to a child, partner or charity, or split it equally. Estate tax applies in countries that have it in real life — [KiwiPoints](https://www.kiwipoints.com/how-to-avoid-estate-tax-in-bitlife/)

### Inferences
- Continuing as a child appears limited in the free tier, since Bitizenship advertises "unlimited generations" (section 1).

### Gaps
- No sourced information on mental health (depression, therapy, psychiatrist) or on plastic surgery botch outcomes. [background, unverified]: botched surgeries lower Looks; doctor types include a regular doctor, experimental treatment and a witch doctor; therapists and psychiatrists are in Mind & Body.
- No full disease list. The wiki page was blocked.

## 6. Ribbons and achievements

### Takeaway
One ribbon is awarded at death, summarizing the life, and it shows on the tombstone. Guide counts range from 25 (old Newsweek) to 30 and to 40 or more, and Candywriter publishes no official list. Achievements number in the hundreds; guide estimates are 157, 282 and "300+", growing with updates.
