# BitLife Monetization, Premium Features, Packs & Challenges (state as of Oct 2026)

Method note: in this research environment, direct page fetches (App Store, Fandom wiki, gaming guides) were blocked by the network proxy. Every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Treat prices as indicative only. They vary by region, platform and date, and sources disagree a lot. Many search results were unofficial "MOD APK" sites (e.g. bitlifemodzapk.com, bitlifemodapkz.com, apkbitlife.com, bittlifeapk.com). Those are unreliable and possibly unsafe, so I excluded their claims unless noted.

Background: BitLife is made by Candywriter, LLC (Miami). Stillfront Group bought Candywriter in April 2020 for about $74.4M upfront, worth up to $195M with earn-outs — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/73190/stillfront-group-acquires-bitlife-developer-candywriter/). Goodgame Studios, another Stillfront studio, co-published the German version (BitLife DE) in Nov 2021 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/77573/goodgame-candywriter-launch-bitlife-german/). The official site is bitlifeapp.com, with "What's New", patch notes and a Help Center — [bitlifeapp.com](https://bitlifeapp.com/).

## Bitizenship: what it includes, price, subscription vs one-time

### Takeaway
Bitizenship is BitLife's core premium upgrade. It is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that removes ads and unlocks "VIP" content. US App Store prices seen in 2026-era listings are about $7.99 (one listing also shows a $9.99 entry). Older guides quote $3.99–$4.99. A cheaper ad-remover-only purchase also exists.

### Cited Findings
- US App Store in-app purchase list (via search snippet): Bitizenship $7.99, God Mode $9.99, Bitizenship + God Mode bundle $15.99, Boss Mode $20.99. The same page also lists a second Bitizenship entry at $9.99, so the listing is internally inconsistent (possibly a regional/legacy SKU) — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536)
- Older guide: Bitizen costs $4.99 USD, "one time purchase, it is not a monthly subscription," and the price has risen since launch. Buyers get all future Bitizen content free — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-bitizen/)
- Escapist Magazine (older article) gives a $12.99 Bitizen + God Mode bundle price. The bundle price has since risen to $15.99 on the US App Store — [Escapist](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/best-bitlife-packs-amp-add-ons-to-buy/); [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536)
- Welsh government parent guide: Bitizenship is "a premium version of the game that must be paid for with real money". It removes ads and offers "VIP" features like exotic pets, unlimited generations and special interactions with characters — [Hwb (gov.wales)](https://hwb.gov.wales/keeping-safe-online/in-the-know/bitlife)
- Bitizen perks named in guides: more events and interactions, mafia events, bands, prison gangs, dark mode, more generations (continue as children), more pets, no ads, a "performance enhancer". Another list adds Pet Store & Breeders, Boss Interactions, Hire Hitmen and Teacher Interactions — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-become-a-bitizen-in-bitlife/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-bitizen/)
- There is a separate ad-remover purchase. It removes ads "in the same way as Bitizen" but without the other features — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-bitizen/)
- Purchases can be restored via Settings → "Restore Earlier Purchase" (bottom of the settings page), including from another device — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/why-is-my-bitlife-purchase-not-working/)

### Inferences
- The price trend runs from about $3.99 at launch, to $4.99, to $7.99 (US, 2025–26 listings). The parent-written report should quote "about $7.99 USD, varies by region".
- No evidence of a recurring BitLife subscription ("BitLife+" or similar) appeared in any source. Monetization still looks built on one-time unlocks, consumables and ads.

### Gaps
- I could not see the exact current price of the standalone ad remover.
- I could not verify a current, official, complete list of Bitizen perks. The lists above come from third-party guides of varying age.
- I found no evidence for or against a subscription tier launched in 2026. The official store page could not be fetched.

## God Mode (and later updates)

### Takeaway
God Mode is a one-time purchase, added in version 1.33. It lets you edit names, appearance and stats (including hidden ones) for your character and other people, and set royalty status. Current US price is about $9.99. Older quotes range from $2.99 to $8.99.

### Cited Findings
- Introduced in BitLife version 1.33. You can edit Athleticism, Discipline and other hidden stats, plus Fertility and Karma — [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-god-mode-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-god-mode-and-other-new-features-in-bitlife-version-1-33/)
- You can edit anyone's name, looks and stats, change your appearance, and change royalty status in countries with a monarchy — [BitLife Wiki – God Mode](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/God_Mode); [Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/god-mode-bitlife/)
- You can customize a character before birth through the Appearance tab (facial features, hairstyle). Appearance editing applies only in the 21–44 age range. You cannot change birthday, siblings, pet color or conception method — [Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/god-mode-bitlife/)
- Location in-game: Main Menu → BitLife Marketplace → BitLife Essentials → God Mode — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/why-is-my-bitlife-purchase-not-working/)
- Prices quoted: US App Store $9.99 (Bitizen + God Mode bundle $15.99), wiki $8.99 (old), Escapist "$7.99", other guides $4.99 or $2.99. Players reported God Mode was briefly offered free for a limited time in March 2020 — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536); [Escapist](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/best-bitlife-packs-amp-add-ons-to-buy/); [AppleVis forum](https://applevis.com/comment/110600)

### Inferences
- Custom people and NPC control: the sources support editing other characters' names, looks and stats. They do not support direct "control" of NPCs' actions.

### Gaps
- I found no source confirming a "God Mode 2" or a major 2025–26 God Mode overhaul.

## Time Machine, Boss Mode, special Modes, other premium purchases

### Takeaway
The Time Machine is a consumable you pay for per use (about $0.99 per use in the US), with an "Unlimited Time Machine" one-time SKU in some stores. Boss Mode (about $20.99 US) unlocks all current and future Job Packs, but not Expansion Packs. Newer premium "Modes", such as Vampire Mode and Ultimate Fighter Mode, are separate one-time purchases at about $10–12.

### Cited Findings
- Time Machine: the US listing shows "Time Machine Use $0.99" (per use). The Mexico storefront shows Time Machine MXN $19.00 and Unlimited Time Machine MXN $299.00. 2019 players said you pay each time you use it — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/app/1374403536); [App Store MX (BitLife GO!)](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/bitlife-go-life-simulator/id6738096599?l=en-GB); [AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/98514)
- The Time Machine lets you go back to an earlier age and make different choices, e.g. after death. GameSkinny calls the unlimited Time Machine the better buy for frequent users — [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/culture/top-10-best-bitlife-expansions-and-job-packs-worth-buying/)
- Official Help Center: "Boss Mode is a one-time purchase that unlocks all current and future Job Packs including Mafia, Pro Athlete, Musician, Actor, and more!" — [BitLife Zendesk](https://bitlife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5165069804948-What-is-Boss-Mode)
- Official BitLife X account: "Boss Mode includes all special careers! Investor and Landlord are expansion packs." This means Expansion Packs are NOT included in Boss Mode — [BitLife on X](https://x.com/BitLifeApp/status/1637882951894482945?lang=en)
- Boss Mode prices: $20.99 (US App Store), $19.99 (TikTok/2024-ish), $15.99 (Escapist and fan wiki thread, older) — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bitlife-life-simulator/id1374403536); [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/is-boss-mode-bitlife-worth-getting); [Escapist](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/best-bitlife-packs-amp-add-ons-to-buy/); [Fandom thread](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000071687)
- Ultimate Fighter Mode is officially "a one-time purchase that unlocks the full combat career experience" and stays unlocked across every character. A fan guide says about $10 US — [bitlifeapp.com](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new/ultimate-fighter-mode); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/complete-bitlife-ultimate-fighter-mode-guide-leagues-move-set-stamina-more/)
- Vampire Mode is an official one-time purchase. A guide says about $10–12 US — [bitlifeapp.com](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new/vampire-mode); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/complete-bitlife-vampire-guide-progeny-hunt-more/)
- One comparison page lists the main BitLife app as having 10 in-app purchase SKUs on the App Store (the App Store shows only the top 10) — [AppPricingLab](https://apppricinglab.com/compare/apple/1374403536/479431697)

### Inferences
- The Marketplace has four tiers: essentials (Bitizenship, God Mode, bundle), Boss Mode (job packs), individual Expansion Packs and "Modes" (Vampire, Ultimate Fighter, Movie Director, etc.), and consumables (Time Machine uses).

### Gaps
- I could not verify a current official list of every Job Pack that Boss Mode includes. The official source names Mafia, Pro Athlete, Musician and Actor "and more". Third-party lists add Supermodel/Model, Politician, Astronaut, Business, Secret Agent, Street Hustler and "The Dealer" (v3.14.12) — [ezechax](https://ezechax.club/bitlife-job-packs/); [Games Learning Society](https://www.gameslearningsociety.org/bitlife-job-packs-update/)
- No sources found for "Cash packs" (buying in-game money) as a current SKU.

## Weekly challenges, events, mini-games, currency

### Takeaway
Weekly Challenges are themed multi-task goals (often tied to holidays or pop culture). In community lore they launch on Saturdays, and completing one earns a one-off themed in-game item. Ribbons are a separate system awarded at death. I found no reliable evidence of a premium in-game currency ("Bitbucks") as of 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Weekly challenges are themed objectives released weekly, sometimes tied to holidays, movies or YouTubers. Completing one earns an in-game item that varies per challenge. A new challenge reportedly launches every Saturday at 3pm EST — [Pro Game Guides – past challenges](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/all-past-challenges-and-requirements-in-bitlife/); [BitLife Wiki (fan site)](https://bitlifewiki.com/)
- Ribbons are awarded when a life ends and shown on the gravestone. One guide counts about 40. Examples: Gold Digger (marry without a prenup and gain $1.5M), Houdini (escape prison 10+ times), Big Boss (net worth over $20M at death) — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-earn-every-ribbon-in-bitlife/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-ribbons-list-how-to-get-all-ribbons/); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/how-to-get-all-ribbons-in-bitlife-complete-bitlife-ribbons-list/)
- Mode-specific mechanics: Ultimate Fighter Mode adds leagues, a move set and stamina. Vampire Mode adds progeny and hunting — [Pro Game Guides UFM](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/complete-bitlife-ultimate-fighter-mode-guide-leagues-move-set-stamina-more/); [Pro Game Guides Vampire](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/complete-bitlife-vampire-guide-progeny-hunt-more/)

### Inferences
- Challenge rewards are cosmetic or collectible items (e.g. special in-game items or accessories), not currency.

### Gaps
- No reliable source found on: a premium currency ("Bitbucks" or similar), daily events, stamps, or the exact reward types for 2026 challenges.
- I could not get specifics on mini-games (prison escape maze, burglary/heist, Mind & Body activities). They are widely known in-game but no source was retrieved here.
- I could not confirm 2026 seasonal events beyond the Movie Director Film Festival sweepstakes.
