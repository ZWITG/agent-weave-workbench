# BitLife: History, Update Timeline, Platforms, and Spin-offs (as of late October 2026)

> Method note: in this session, WebFetch and direct HTTP access were blocked by the proxy (DNS/CONNECT refused for Wikipedia, Fandom, Google Play, and others). All findings below come from web-search result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Dates come from the snippets. Where sources disagree, both are given. Treat exact days and version numbers as approximate unless they come from an official BitLife or Candywriter post.

## 1. Origin, developer, platforms, business milestones, controversies

### Takeaway
Candywriter LLC (founded 2006 by Kevin O'Neil and Nadir Khan) released BitLife on iOS on 29 September 2018 and on Android on 5 February 2019. Stillfront Group bought Candywriter in 2020: $74.4M upfront, up to $195M with earn-outs. BitLife is still a free-to-play mobile game supported by ads and in-app purchases. I found no evidence of a Netflix Games edition, and a Nintendo 3DS version was cancelled.

### Cited Findings
- Candywriter was founded in 2006 by Kevin O'Neil and Nadir Khan. BitLife launched in 2018 as the studio's "latest unique mash-up game." — [Medium/SEgames summary of the Stillfront acquisition](https://medium.com/@SEgames/stillfront-group-acquires-bitlife-developer-candywriter-92eb08532a5d)
- iOS release: 29 September 2018. Android release: 5 February 2019. — [BitLife Wiki (Fandom), BitLife: Life Simulator](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/BitLife:_Life_Simulator); [HandWiki](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Software:BitLife)
- Stillfront acquired 100% of Candywriter in April 2020: $37.5M in new shares plus $36.9M in cash upfront (about $74.4M), with annual earn-outs taking the maximum to $195M. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/73190/stillfront-group-acquires-bitlife-developer-candywriter/); [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/stillfront-group-acquires-casual-game-maker-candywriter-for-74-4-million); [Stillfront press release (23 Apr 2020)](https://www.stillfront.com/en/stillfront-group-acquires-candywriter-llc-and-discloses-updated-pro-forma-figures-for-2019/)
- 2019 financials: Candywriter revenue of about USD 26M with an EBIT margin of about 59%. Over 62% of BitLife revenue came from in-game advertising. At acquisition there were about 42M downloads to date (40M+ from BitLife, about 70% organic). — [Stillfront acquisition PDF](https://www.stillfront.com/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/04/stillfront-stillfront-group-acquires-candywriter-llc-and-discloses-updated-pro-forma-figures-for-2-200423.pdf); [Medium/SEgames](https://medium.com/@SEgames/stillfront-group-acquires-bitlife-developer-candywriter-92eb08532a5d)
- Android download figures: AppBrain shows 50,000,000+ installs on Google Play, about 61M in total, and about 650K in the last 30 days (snapshot taken roughly October 2026). — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/bitlife-life-simulator/com.candywriter.bitlife)
- Sensor Tower's monthly estimates (around 300K downloads a month, with revenue estimates ranging from $300K to $600K) are inconsistent between its iOS and Android pages. — [Sensor Tower iOS](https://app.sensortower.com/ios/us/candywriter-llc/app/bitlife-life-simulator/1374403536/); [Sensor Tower Android](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/com.candywriter.bitlife?country=US)
- Candywriter is part of Stillfront Group, not Netflix. — [Candywriter About](https://candywriter.com/about/)
- Other platforms: a Nintendo 3DS version of BitLife was planned and cancelled. — [Cancelled Games Wiki](https://cancelled-games.fandom.com/wiki/BitLife_(Nintendo_3DS))
- Localized standalone apps existed alongside the main app, including BitLife Español, BitLife DE, and BitLife BR. — [App Store, BitLife Español](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1607840241); [App Store, BitLife DE](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/bitlife-de-lebenssimulation/id1570661587); [Uptodown, BitLife BR](https://bitlife-br.en.uptodown.com/android/versions)
- In August 2026, Candywriter announced it was folding the language apps into the main app ("One app. Every language."). French and Portuguese apps leave the stores in September 2026, Español in October 2026, and German later in 2026. Saved lives and purchases do not transfer from the old apps. — [BitLife What's New, One app. Every language.](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new/one-app-every-language-your-life-choices-just-got-international)
- Controversy (accessibility): Candywriter's legal agents sent a DMCA notice that forced AppleVis to remove its BitLife directory entries. Candywriter later said AppleVis had been wrongly flagged as an "APK website," and the entries were restored. The post is about two years old, so roughly 2024. — [AppleVis blog](https://www.applevis.com/blog/updated-now-resolved-candywriters-betrayal-blind-low-vision-gaming-community-announcing-forced)
- Controversy (monetization): fans complain that Boss Mode was sold with promises of future content, and that paid expansion packs keep adding costs. A Change.org petition asks Stillfront to sell BitLife to a new owner. These are advocacy and opinion sources. — [Gnostalgic](https://www.gnostalgic.org/bitlife.php); [Change.org petition](https://www.change.org/p/make-stillfront-sell-bitlife-to-a-new-owner-who-actually-cares-about-the-players)
- Accessibility regressions continued into 2026. An August 2026 fix restored accessibility features, but marketplace accessibility bugs and purchase-restoration problems are still listed as open in "On Our Radar." — [BitLife What's New](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new)

### Inferences
- The business runs mainly on ads, with paid add-ons layered on top: Bitizenship, God Mode, Time Machine, Boss Mode, and since 2023 a steady series of paid expansion packs and "Modes." Those paid packs are the main source of the community friction.
- The 2026 consolidation of language apps and the removal of Seasons (section 4) suggest Candywriter is simplifying the product under Stillfront.

### Gaps
- Netflix Games edition: several searches found no evidence that a Netflix Games version of BitLife exists. Treat any claim of one as unverified.
- I found no official PC or web version. "Play online" and "unblocked" sites such as bitlifefree.online look unofficial.
- I found no reliable lifetime revenue figure or cross-platform download total after 2020.
- A purported former-employee lawsuit appeared only on a suspicious domain, so I excluded it.
- I found no source on mature-content or age-rating controversies.

## 2. Chronological timeline of major updates

### Takeaway
BitLife began with version 1.x in 2018–2020: Fame (April 2019), Weekly Challenges (February 2020), Royalty (September 2020), Mafia (December 2020). It then moved to a paid expansion-pack model on version 3.x: Landlord (March 2023), Secret Agent (about 2024), Zookeeper (2024), Casino (November 2024), Political (2024), Vampire Mode (October 2025), Ultimate Fighter Mode (June 2026), Movie Director (September 2026). Around September 2026 the app was on version 3.25–3.26.

## 3. Spin-offs and related apps

### Takeaway
Candywriter's confirmed spin-offs are DogLife: BitLife Dogs (announced 30 Sep 2021; Android 1 Nov 2021, iOS 3 Nov 2021) and CatLife: BitLife Cats (3 Jan 2022). Both are text-based life simulators played as a pet, on iOS and Android, with purchases separate from BitLife. I found no multiplayer or 3D spin-off.

### Cited Findings
- DogLife was announced on 30 Sep 2021 as BitLife's first spin-off and released on Android on 1 Nov 2021 and iOS on 3 Nov 2021. — [BitLife Wiki, DogLife](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/DogLife); [DogLife Wiki](https://doglife.fandom.com/wiki/DogLife:_BitLife_Dogs); [App Store, BitLife Dogs: DogLife](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1589129140); [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DogLife)
- CatLife launched on 3 Jan 2022 on the App Store and Google Play as the second spin-off. Purchases from BitLife or DogLife do not carry over. — [BitLife Wiki, CatLife](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/CatLife); [Try Hard Guides](https://tryhardguides.com/catlife-has-just-released-for-apple-and-android-devices/)
- BitLife teased a pet-based mini-game that hinted at something bigger, which preceded DogLife. — [GamerJournalist](https://gamerjournalist.com/bitlife-teases-mini-game-update/)

### Inferences
- No new spin-off has appeared since 2022. Development effort seems to have gone into BitLife's paid packs instead.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of a BitLife multiplayer mode, a 3D BitLife, or any other spin-off through October 2026.
- I could not confirm the detailed feature lists or current update status of DogLife and CatLife.

## 4. Community

### Takeaway
The community centers on weekly in-game challenges (since February 2020, with past challenges replayable through a paid Challenge Vault), guide sites, the Fandom wiki, YouTube creators (CallMeKevin's long-running BitLife series), and BitLife's X and TikTok accounts. Players have pushed back over monetization and accessibility.

### Cited Findings
- Weekly challenges are themed goal lists that award an in-game item. The first was the Alphabet Challenge on 22 Feb 2020. Sources disagree on when they start (Saturdays at 3pm EST vs. Sunday, running about 5 days). The Challenge Vault costs about $4.99 and unlocks past challenges. — [GamerJournalist](https://gamerjournalist.com/bitlife-challenges-all-past-challenges-and-requirements); [Pro Game Guides, best weekly challenges](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/best-bitlife-weekly-challenges-active-and-vaulted/)
- Challenge themes include holidays, movies, and popular YouTubers. Examples are "Mischief Managed" and "Deadbeat Dad." — [DualShockers, Mischief Managed](https://www.dualshockers.com/bitlife-mischief-managed-challenge-guide-how-to-insult-your-principal-pirate-porches-break-out-of-juvie/); [DualShockers, Deadbeat Dad](https://www.dualshockers.com/bitlife-deadbeat-dad-challenge-guide-how-to-never-have-a-full-time-job-gamble-money-have-home-repossessed-reconnect-with-an-estranged-child/)
- CallMeKevin's "BitLife" YouTube series is catalogued as a multi-season show on TheTVDB. — [TheTVDB](https://www.thetvdb.com/series/bitlife-callmekevin/allseasons/official)
- The Fandom wiki covers updates, expansion packs, and spin-offs. — [BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/BitLife_-_Life_Simulator_Wiki); [Category: Expansion Packs](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Expansion_Packs)
- Official channels include @BitLifeApp on X and a TikTok presence that teases upcoming updates. — [BitLife on X](https://x.com/bitlifeapp?lang=en); [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/bitlife-upcoming-updates)
- Blind and low-vision players are an organized group on AppleVis, and they push on accessibility. — [AppleVis](https://www.applevis.com/blog/updated-now-resolved-candywriters-betrayal-blind-low-vision-gaming-community-announcing-forced); [AppleVis comment warning about an upcoming version](https://applevis.com/comment/132456)
- Frustration over late or paywalled content led to a petition asking Stillfront to sell BitLife. — [Gnostalgic](https://www.gnostalgic.org/bitlife.php); [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/make-stillfront-sell-bitlife-to-a-new-owner-who-actually-cares-about-the-players)
