## Regular jobs, part-time jobs, freelance gigs, internships, promotions, job performance, retirement and pensions

### Takeaway
Work unlocks by age: freelance gigs at about 13 to 14, part-time jobs at 14, more part-time options at 16, and the full job list at 18. In a job you can work harder, ask for a raise, request more hours or resign, and staying longer makes promotion more likely. Retiring with a pension has historically needed about 20 years of service plus a minimum age. Later updates added retirement plans, work hours and stress, office co-worker and HR interactions, and job interviews.

### Cited Findings
- Freelance gigs open at 13 and part-time jobs at 14 according to one guide; another says both need age 14 (the sources conflict on the freelance age). The full part-time lineup opens at 16 and the whole range of jobs and careers at 18. — [Pro Game Guides: Part-Time Jobs Guide](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-part-time-jobs-guide/); [Touch, Tap, Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-get-part-time-job-in-bitlife/); [WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-full-guide-to-part-time-jobs-and-freelancing/)
- Freelance gigs are one-time jobs where you set your own rate. Part-time jobs carry over from year to year. — [WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-full-guide-to-part-time-jobs-and-freelancing/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-a-part-time-job-in-bitlife/)
- The longer you stay in a part-time job, the more likely a promotion or raise. From the Occupation tab you can request more hours, ask for a raise, resign, or work harder. Full-time jobs give raises or promotions for good performance. — [Careers/Job activities (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Careers/Job_activities); [Gfinity: BitLife jobs](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/jobs)

- Version 1.22, the "Back to School" update, added an intelligence-test mini-game, retirement plans, career collecting, work hours, and stress levels. — [Level Winner: 1.22 guide](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-back-to-school-update-guide-version-1-22-intelligence-test-mini-game-retirement-plans-career-collecting-work-hours-and-stress-levels-explained/)

- Military, police, doctor, lawyer/judge, pilot, firefighter and similar jobs appear to be regular jobs with education requirements, not paid special-career packs. That fits the pack lists below, which do not name them.

## Special careers and pack gating

### Takeaway
Special careers are found under Occupation > Special Careers. Most are paid "Job Packs" (about $4.99 each historically, or bundled in Boss Mode). As of October 2024 the wiki listed ten: Actor, Astronaut, Athlete, Business, Mafia, Musician, Politician, Street Hustler, Model, and Dealer. Newer packs reported since then include a Political Expansion Pack, Ultimate Fighter Mode, and a Movie Director Pack (2026). Royalty comes from your birth country, not from a career pack.

### Cited Findings
- Special Careers is a tab under the Occupation menu. Each career has its own prerequisites, often talents built through lessons or God Mode. — [Pro Game Guides: Special Careers](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-special-careers-in-bitlife/)
- As of October 2024 the Fandom wiki listed ten special career packs: Actor, Astronaut, Athlete, Business, Mafia, Musician, Politician, Street Hustler, Model, and Dealer. They cost $4.99 each unless bought through Boss Mode, which one result put at $11.99. — [Careers/Occupation (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Careers/Occupation)
- Another guide says Boss Mode includes Actor, Musician, Supermodel, Athlete, Politician, and Astronaut. — [Ezechax: job packs](https://ezechax.club/bitlife-job-packs/) (low-quality fan site)
- An older guide named only four special career categories: Musician, Organized Crime, Political Offices, and Professional Sports. All need age 18 or older. Musicians practice an instrument or vocals as a child, then join a band or audition as a solo artist. Politics has four levels, with a campaign needed at each one. — [The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/bitlife-how-to-get-special-careers/)

Astronaut: a guide lists a science or engineering degree and high Smarts. — [Pro Game Guides: Special Careers](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-special-careers-in-bitlife/)

Another says the Politician pack is under Job Packs in the marketplace, or in Boss Mode. Recommended prep is high Looks and Smarts and a Political Science or Law degree, then starting at a local post such as the School Board. Campaigns are self-funded. Bitizenship price is quoted at both $7.99 and $4.99 (sources conflict). — [Pro Game Guides: President](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-how-to-become-president/); [Game Rant: President](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-become-president/); [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-become-president-in-bitlife/)

## Business, stock market, crypto, lottery, gambling/casino, heists

### Takeaway
The Business update (GameFAQs patch notes, 25 industries) lets you found or take over a company through the Business Job Pack. You can create products and manage employees. The Stock Market update added stocks, crypto, bonds, penny stocks, funds, and a hireable financial advisor. The free casino has blackjack and horse racing. The paid Casino Expansion Pack (announced around November 2024) adds roulette, keno, slots, High-Low, and others, for 9 games in total. Heists include bank and train robbery.

### Cited Findings
- The Business update patch notes cover 25 industries. — [GameFAQs: Business Update patch notes](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/news?id=46583)
- The Business Job Pack is bought in the in-game store; one source says Boss Mode includes it. Go to Jobs > Special Careers > Business, where you can found a company or take over an existing one as CEO. — [Prima Games: run a business](https://primagames.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-run-a-successful-business); [Try Hard Guides](https://tryhardguides.com/how-to-start-a-business-in-bitlife/); [bitlifepro: CEO](https://bitlifepro.com/how-to-become-ceo/)
- Products are made through "Create Product" on the company dashboard. Guides advise researching demand and starting production low to avoid over-producing. Employee morale matters; guides suggest competitive pay, bonuses, and recognition. Beginners can try low-competition industries such as gift shops or food trucks; robotics and car manufacturing earn more but need more capital. — [Ezechax: business guide](https://ezechax.com/bitlife-business-guide/) (fan site); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-run-a-successful-business)

## Assets: real estate, vehicles, jewelry, heirlooms, taxes, wills and inheritance

### Takeaway
You can buy houses with cash or a mortgage. Renting out properties needs the paid Landlord Expansion Pack. Flipping means renovating and then selling. Jewelry loses value every year. Heirlooms come from a daily attic mini-game, added in v1.21, and pass to the heir. On death, the next-generation character inherits houses and cars; if under 18, the assets are auctioned and the heir keeps the money. A will can direct assets to a chosen child.

### Cited Findings
- Renting properties needs the paid Landlord Expansion Pack. Tenants pay monthly rent. Upgrades cost money upfront and may lose money at first. Some older sources say BitLife has no rentals, which predates the pack. — [Pro Game Guides: rent property](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-rent-your-property-in-bitlife/); [Prima Games: landlord](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-make-money-as-a-landlord-in-bitlife); [Attack of the Fanboy](https://attackofthefanboy.com/guides/how-to-rent-properties-in-bitlife/)
- Flipping: buy, use Renovate, then sell. The sale price rises with condition and with time held. — [Pro Game Guides: flip houses](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-flip-houses-in-bitlife-and-sell-them-guide/)
- Mortgages: you can buy with cash or a mortgage, and the application is approved only if you have some balance in your account (likely meaning enough for a down payment). — [GameSkinny: buy a house](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-buy-a-house/)
- Jewelry (rings, earrings, pendants, charms) loses value every year even with maintenance. You can appraise, gift, sell or donate it. — [Assets (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Assets)
- Heirlooms come from a daily attic mini-game, count toward net worth, and go to the character chosen for the next generation, unless a will assigns assets to a specific child. — [Heirloom (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Heirloom); [BitLife Zendesk: What are Heirlooms?](https://bitlife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038370612-What-are-Heirlooms); [BitLifeGuides: valuable heirlooms](https://www.bitlifeguides.com/most-valuable-heirlooms-in-bitlife-large-list/)
- Inheritance: the new character inherits the late parent's houses and cars. If under 18, the assets are auctioned and the heir keeps the proceeds. — [Assets (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Assets)
- Family heirlooms were introduced in version 1.21. — [Gamezebo: 1.21 guide](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-21-guide-prison-life-riot-mini-game-family-heirlooms-job-interviews-and-more-explained/)

## Crime: petty crimes, heists, arrest, trial, prison life, death row, organized crime

### Takeaway
Crimes are under Activities > Crimes. They include shoplifting, porch piracy, pickpocketing, burglary, grand theft auto, bank and train robbery, extortion (with the Mafia), embezzlement (v1.31), murder, and contract killing (hiring a hitman). Prison (expanded in v1.21) has prison jobs, bribing guards, gangs (more common in higher-security prisons), a riot mini-game (10+ inmates in the chain wins), and an escape grid mini-game (the guard moves twice per player move). You can appeal with a lawyer, and better lawyers improve your odds. Serial killers almost always get a death sentence.

### Cited Findings
- Mafia-related crimes on the wiki: Bank Robbery, Burglary, Extortion, Grand Theft Auto, Pickpocket, Porch Pirate, Shoplift, and Train Robbery. In extortion you force a business owner to pay; if they refuse you can scare, whack, or shake them down. — [Mafia (Fandom wiki)](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia)
- Porch Pirate (stealing mail or packages from porches) and Shoplift (stealing from a mall) came with a criminal update. Pickpocket, Porch Pirate and Shoplift are low-stakes. — [Level Winner: 1.29 Criminal update](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-1-29-update-guide-how-to-mess-up-your-bitizens-lives-in-bitlife-version-1-29-aka-the-criminal-update/); [Happy Gamer: Android 1.22.1](https://happygamer.com/bitlife-for-android-follows-prison-update-by-adding-more-criminal-activities-in-update-1-22-1-56640/)
- The game already had theft, grand theft auto, drug trafficking and murder; an update added Contract Killing (hiring a hitman). — [Happy Gamer](https://happygamer.com/bitlife-for-android-follows-prison-update-by-adding-more-criminal-activities-in-update-1-22-1-56640/)
- Embezzlement was added in v1.31. — [Gamezebo: 1.31](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-31-update-guide-weekly-challenges-relationship-counselling-embezzlement-and-more/)
- The Sly Duper challenge involves bank robbery and porch piracy. — [Game Rant: Sly Duper](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-complete-sly-duper-challenge-rob-bank-porch-cops/); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/bitlife/news/how-to-complete-the-sly-duper-challenge-in-bitlife)
- Prison update v1.21 added prison jobs, the riot mini-game, bribing guards, and joining gangs. Riot: swipe to chain inmates; guards appear once the chain reaches 7; 10 or more makes the riot succeed.
