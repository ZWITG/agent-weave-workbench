# 01 Investor Memo

*Purpose:* The two-page case for investing in Relivable. Send it before the first meeting.

> [!note] Working title and estimates
> **Relivable** is a working title pending trademark clearance. Market figures are cited third-party estimates checked on 26 Oct 2026. Financial figures are our planning estimates, not forecasts. Relivable is an original game inspired by the text life-sim genre. It is not affiliated with BitLife or Candywriter, and it uses no copied text, names, UI or assets.

## In one line
A text life simulator for phones and PCs, in which every choice is remembered. It keeps what millions love about the genre (birth to death, one tap a year, wild choices, generations), and it is built to be fairer, clearer and more shareable than the genre's tired leader. **It is already playable.**

## The problem
- **One game owns a profitable niche, and its players are unhappy.** BitLife has 50M+ Google Play installs and brought its developer about $26M of revenue at a 59% EBIT margin in 2019. It still earns roughly US$9–11M a quarter for Stillfront ([[02 Market and Competitors]]).
- **Its growth now depends on paid ads.** Organic revenue fell 19% in each of Q1 and Q2 2026 when paid user acquisition was cut, then stabilised at about -2% in Q3.
- **Players keep naming the same complaints:** intrusive ads, new systems sold as paid packs (up to a $20.99 Boss Mode), lost purchases and saves, repetitive events, and accessibility regressions ([[03 Player Research and Wishlist]]).
- **The bigger alternatives have left the field.** The Sims Mobile closed in January 2026, and Life by You was cancelled in 2024.

## The product
Relivable is built on five pillars:
1. **Every choice is remembered.** People hold grudges, and consequences come back years later.
2. **One tap gets to anything.** There's a search bar, and locked actions say why they're locked.
3. **Fair forever.** All careers are free, ads are capped and opt-in, and there's no paying per use to undo a choice.
4. **Share your story.** Wrapped-style Life Recap cards, dynasties and seed codes.
5. **Accessible by default.** Screen readers, large text and colour-vision modes.

What makes it distinct on screen: **the game changes its whole look with the life you lead.** It becomes Yard Time in prison, Gilded among the super-rich, and one of 14 career worlds (velvet curtains for actors, deep space for astronauts, a ring under the lights for fighters).

## The proof: built in under three weeks
Development started on 26 Oct 2026. By 13 Nov 2026 (details in [[05 Product and Traction]]):
- **A complete, playable game** on Android (it updates itself), Windows, iPhone/iPad and the web.
- **About 83,200 lines of engine and UI code** and **180 automated tests**, including a 500-life simulator and a release fuzz test of 40,000+ simulated years.
- **Content and systems:**
  - Over 200 story events and scene families.
  - 30 mini-games.
  - 14 special careers, each with its own screen, look, people and Life Recap page.
  - Crime, heists, prison and manhunts.
  - An upper-class "Echelon".
  - Eight-generation dynasties.
  - A living world with its own newspaper.
  - Money shown in 40+ currencies.
- **Ready-made marketing:** an 80-second promo, two Instagram batches (feed posts, carousels, stories and Reels), all made from real gameplay.

This is a **capital-efficiency** story. The plan budgeted ~$25–60K and 8 weeks for this prototype. A founder working with AI-assisted engineering delivered more than the planned prototype at a fraction of that cost. **[OWNER: cash spent to date.]**

**What is not proven yet:** no real players have played it. Retention, monetisation and organic sharing are still hypotheses. The first job of this round is to measure them.

## The market and the opening
- **The niche pays.** Life Simulator is among the top-earning hybrid-casual subgenres, with US Android ARPU of about $11–15 (Appodeal 2025).
- **Distribution costs are falling.** US iOS link-out purchases carry 0% Apple commission while a 15% rate awaits court approval, and Google allows alternative billing from June 2026.
- **Regulation is moving towards fair design.** Loot-box rules and the EU Digital Fairness Act reward transparent monetisation, which is what Relivable has by design.

## Business model
Relivable is free to play. There are **no loot boxes, no paid currency and no pay-per-use undo.** Revenue comes from:
- capped, opt-in rewarded ads;
- **Relivable Plus**, a one-time ~$9.99 unlock (no ads, longer dynasties, unlimited Rewind);
- a ~$4.99 cosmetic and story Season Pass;
- $2.99–3.99 Story Packs;
- cosmetics and two bundles.

Plan base case: blended ARPDAU of about **$0.066**, split roughly 57:43 between purchases and ads ([[06 Monetization and Economy]]).

So **growth is planned to be organic and creator-led**: share cards, Reels and creators, with paid ads only where they pay back.

## Competition and why we win
- **BitLife** is the incumbent: mature, monetised by paywalls, and losing goodwill.
- **Other life sims** (InstaLife, Life Simulator clones) copy BitLife's structure.
- **Visual sims** have retreated.

Our edge:
1. **Memory and depth.** The engine is built for consequences, not random events.
2. **Visual identity.** Looks that change with your life, unusual for text games and native to short-video marketing.
3. **A fairness stance** that players have been petitioning for.
4. **Cost to build.** A small team with AI tooling ships content faster than a traditional studio.
