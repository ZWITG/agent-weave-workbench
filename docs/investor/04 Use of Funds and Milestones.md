## 2. Why the budget is lean
- The plan's **Low** budget assumes a founder-led team with offshore or contract help. That is how the game has actually been built so far. The prototype the plan priced at $25–60K is already done.
- **Writing, not engineering, is the critical path** ([[11 Roadmap Team and Budget]] §6). The MVP needs 1,500+ approved events and launch needs 3,000+. The money mostly buys writers, editing and art. Engineering is largely AI-assisted under the founder's review.
- We did **not** cut the plan's line items for legal work, localisation, the accessibility audit or launch marketing.

## 4. If a gate is missed
| Gate result | What happens to the money |
|---|---|
| **Gate 2 iterate or stop** | Tranche 1 spend pauses after playtests (under $30K spent). We redesign, or return the unspent funds as the terms allow. **[OWNER/legal]** |
| **Gate 4 iterate** (for example D1 30–35%) | A 4–8 week iteration from tranche 1 contingency, then a fresh Gate 4 readout. Tranche 2 stays held. |
| **Gate 4 stop** | Tranche 2 is not released. The game can continue as a small premium or organic title without paid UA. |
| **Gate 5 below target** | UA is cut back to organic and the LiveOps team is right-sized (the conservative case assumes $40K a month). |
