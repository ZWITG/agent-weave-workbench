# 00 Investor Pack Home

*Purpose:* Everything needed to present Relivable to investors: what to send before a meeting, what to show in it, and what to have ready for due diligence afterwards.

> [!warning] Before this goes to any investor
> 1. **Fill the owner fields.** Items marked **[OWNER]** (founder names, company entity, cash spent to date, round terms) are facts only you can supply. Nothing in this pack invents them.
> 2. **Settle the name.** "RELIVABLE" is a registered US trademark owned by someone else (open item A7 in [[01 Phase 2 Open Items]]). Get IP counsel's view before the name goes on a deck that circulates, or present it as a working title, which this pack does.
> 3. **Re-check market figures.** The market and benchmark figures were checked on 26 Oct 2026. Check them again before a term sheet.
> 4. **This is not legal or financial advice.** Round structure, valuation and securities rules (who you may pitch, and how) need a lawyer.
