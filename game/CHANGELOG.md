# Relivable: major updates

Named updates since the game was created (26 October 2026). Newest last.

## 1.0 "First Breath" (26 Oct)
- The first playable Flutter version of Relivable, with its dark "Night Feed" look.
- A Windows desktop build and installer.
- Money shown in the header, and swipe up to open Activities (45 activities).
- Relationships with bond, memories and grudges.
- Original sound effects.

## 1.1 "Playtime" (27 Oct)
- The first activity mini-games, and options on the Path tab.
- Results shown as pop-ups.
- The Wealth tab economy.
- Auto-updates on Android phones.

## 1.2 "Living World" (27 Oct)
- World events and a newspaper.
- People in your life get their own news.
- Richer event writing.

## 1.3 "Whole Life" (the Waves) (27 Oct)
- **A:** a full lifespan, birthday summaries, Rewind and settings.
- **B:** school.
- **C:** deeper careers.
- **D:** love, children and dynasties.
- **E:** money, homes, crime and health.
- **F:** identity, countries, fame, social media, pets, achievements, goals and accessibility.
- **G:**
  - heists, lawsuits and prison riots;
  - funerals and Life Recap cards;
  - heirlooms, collectibles, boats, planes and disasters;
  - fostering, surrogacy, grandchildren, the seasons and 61 new stories.

## 1.4 "Pocket & Shop" (27–30 Oct)
- iPhone and iPad version, plus a home-screen web app.
- A proper shop.
- Every tab made to work like Activities.

## 1.5 "Smooth Sailing" (30 Oct)
- Events flow one at a time.
- Every screen fits small phones and large text.
- More choices in activities, events and people.

## 1.6 "Behind Bars" (30 Oct)
- Prison gangs, visits, yard trading, courses, solitary confinement, new escapes and life after release.
- Crimes as planned jobs, with a target, an approach and twists.
- Street skill and lying low.
- Murder.
- Keeping cars you steal.

## 1.7 "Headlines" (30 Oct)
- A big-news pop-up after each birthday.
- The odds of everything hidden from the player.
- News that affects your life and doesn't repeat itself.
- Friends you make join People.

## 1.8 "Game Night" (30 Oct)
- 8 new mini-games, dressed as each activity.
- Redo everyday activities up to three times a year, with smaller gains each time.

## 1.9 "Big Screen" (30 Oct)
- A desktop layout with tabs down the side and Activities docked on the right.
- Everything you own shown together on the Wealth tab.

## 2.0 "Home & Hustle" (31 Oct)
- 90 more shop items and two new departments.
- Landlord portfolios, moving house, remortgaging and finding tenants.
- Cards for things you own expand to show their options.
- Tap your money to open Wealth.

## 2.1 "Spotlight" (31 Oct)
- Fame reworked, with public image, fame tiers and celebrity life.
- A fame badge under your money.
- Social media moved into Activities.

## 2.2 "Passport" (31 Oct)
- 10 new countries, with researched living conditions.
- 17 new sound effects.
- Moving abroad uses a drop-down list.

## 2.3 "Road to the Underground" (31 Oct)
- A music career: write and release songs (and name them yourself).
- Songwriting is a skill, and hits depend on quality.
- Fame from music once you're established.

## 2.4 "Upper Echelon" (31 Oct)
- Money in local currencies.
- A hidden stage at US$100M with islands, yachts, sports teams, space flights, a billionaire milestone and secret achievements.

## 2.5 "Empire" (31 Oct)
- Run up to five businesses, each with its own trade, upgrades, signature move and events.
- Drop-down lists across the game.

## 2.6 "Clean Sweep" (31 Oct)
- An automated player that plays thousands of lives to find logic errors.
- Fixed: exes deleted from your People, stale "married" status, keeping your job in prison, and more.

## 2.7 "Family Matters" (31 Oct)
- Surprise pregnancies, birth plans, varied births, twins and triplets, and placing a baby for adoption with a reunion later.
- Funerals and wills, honeymoons, being fired, grandchildren, step-parents.
- Grief and other big moments now last for years.
- Teen pregnancy removed.

## 2.8 "Your Way" (31 Oct)
- 18 everyday activities now ask how you want to do them (diets, nights out, tattoos, raises, tours and more).
- Realistic lottery odds.

## 2.9 "Underworld" (31 Oct)
- Heat you can see and manage.
- Dirty money and six ways to launder it.
- Three street crews with ranks, informing and takeovers.
- 7 new crimes.
- A friendlier Crime tab grouped into sections.

## 2.10 "Curtain Up" (31 Oct)
- An opening animation when the game starts or is opened again: the ZWITG mark (letters settle, the red line draws across), then the Relivable logo, then into the game.
- Tap to skip; shorter and calmer with Reduce motion on.
- Shareable intro videos in `media/` (square 1080×1080 and vertical 1080×1920), rebuilt with `tool/make_intro_video.sh`.

## 2.11 "Tidy Up" (31 Oct)
- The Identity, Memories and Collections tiles are hidden from the Me tab.
- Rewind is now a small button beside Age +1 (orange when a rewind is available, grey when not; hidden for Ironman lives) instead of a Me tab tile.
- On very narrow phones the search box shrinks to its icon so everything fits.

## 2.12 "No Papers" (31 Oct)
- Cross into another country without a visa: overstay as a tourist (safest), pay a smuggler (could be a scam) or hide in a lorry or boat (cheap and dangerous).
- Life without papers: only cash-in-hand jobs at lower pay, no bank loans, and immigration checks where you can run, claim asylum or go home.
- A criminal conviction means deportation; a deportation on file makes future visas harder.
- Get legal: apply for papers (strong through marriage, fair after years of work) or wait for an amnesty after five years.

## 2.13 "Joined Up" (31 Oct)
- No papers, no free public healthcare; the mortgage refusal now says why.
- Famous people's convictions hit their image and followers.
- Your crew looks after you in prison (and word gets out about informants); moving abroad or being deported ends your crew membership.
- Funerals while you are in prison: ask for day release, write a letter, or grieve alone.
- Businesses slip while their owner is in prison; crews can lean on business owners for protection money.
- The big-news pop-up now catches deportations, amnesties, asylum, crew promotions, billionaire news and grandchildren.

## 2.14 "Living History" (31 Oct)
- New world news that changes play: border crackdowns, amnesties, police crackdowns on organised crime, tourist booms and market frenzies.
- The world reaches the newer systems: amnesties give papers; crackdowns make crossings and checks riskier; policing raises suspicion of dirty money; crew rackets follow the economy; tourists fill cafés; a pandemic empties restaurants, cancels tours and favours apps; market frenzies boost insider trading and crypto.
- An infamous family name gets you noticed by crews.
- Your biggest moves go into the world's history and your family's reputation: billionaires, the super-rich, foundations, space flights, crime bosses, secret witnesses, business empires, local music stars and deportations.
