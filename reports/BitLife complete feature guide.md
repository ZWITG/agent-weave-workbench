# BitLife Grows From Text Sim to Pack Store

BitLife is Candywriter's free-to-play, text-based life simulator. The official site calls it "The Original Text-Based Life Simulator" ([bitlifeapp.com](https://bitlifeapp.com/)). It launched on iOS on **29 September 2018** and on Android on **5 February 2019** ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/BitLife:_Life_Simulator)). You play one randomly generated or custom-built person from birth to death, one "Age +1" tap per year. Four visible stats drive the game: Happiness, Health, Smarts and Looks. Hidden attributes such as Karma, Willpower, Discipline, Fertility and Craziness sit underneath. One tab holds Activities, and it keeps growing: education, careers, crime, prison, romance, children, fame, pets, assets and generational inheritance. By late October 2026 the game is around **version 3.25–3.26** under Stillfront Group, which bought Candywriter in 2020 for about **$74.4M upfront, up to $195M** with earn-outs ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/73190/stillfront-group-acquires-bitlife-developer-candywriter/)). Since 2023 the game's growth has come mostly from paid content. On top of the old one-time unlocks (Bitizenship, God Mode, Boss Mode, per-use Time Machine) sit roughly a dozen expansion packs and "Modes": Landlord, Investor, C.U.L.T., Black Market, Secret Agent, Zoo, Casino, Outdoors, Political, Vampire, Ultimate Fighter, and the September 2026 Movie Director pack. Free live operations rest on weekly challenges and events. **Confidence caveat:** all five research notes behind this report came from search-result snippets, because direct fetches of the Fandom wiki, App Store, Google Play and guide sites were blocked. Many sources are fan guides from 2019–2024. Prices, counts and age gates below are "last documented" figures, not verified late-2026 values, and conflicting figures are flagged where they appear.

## A one-tap-per-year loop built on four stats and many hidden ones

You start a life from the main menu's "New Life" screen. It can roll a random person or open a "Custom People" builder for characters based on yourself, friends or fictional figures ([Ark Times blog](https://blog.wordpress.arktimes.com/?p=8452)). The Pride update (v1.38, June 2020) added a random chance of being born to two dads or two moms ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-pride-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-bitlife-version-1-38-aka-the-pride-update/)). Version 1.18 added a **Scenario Creator**, new countries, cities and vacations ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-18-1-guide-scenario-creator-new-countries-cities-and-vacations-and-monk-and-nun-careers-explained/)). The research did not turn up how scenario starts work today. The exact creation fields (name, gender, country and city pickers) are well known in-game, but no source confirmed them for 2026.

Health sets lifespan and resistance to illness ([playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)). Smarts sets starting school grades, and Looks gates social outcomes such as cliques. Most stats run **0–100** ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943)). Beneath them is a layer that God Mode can expose and edit. **Karma** rises and falls with good and bad deeds and reportedly affects how long you live. **Willpower**, added with the God Mode overhaul, governs resisting temptations like heavy drinking or cheating. **Discipline** runs from underachiever to overachiever ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=32943); [Prima Games](https://vip-develop.primagames.com/?p=64046)). God Mode also edits Athleticism and Fertility ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-god-mode-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-god-mode-and-other-new-features-in-bitlife-version-1-33/)), and players report a **Craziness** attribute on children, grandchildren and siblings ([AppleVis](https://www.applevis.com/comment/110817)). In 2026, Ultimate Fighter Mode patch notes tied drug-test detection to a "**Wiliness**" stat, so the hidden layer is still growing ([BitLife Patch Notes](https://bitlifeapp.com/whats-new/patch-notes/)).

The game is turn-based. Each Age +1 advances a year and can trigger random event popups. You can use Activities **an unlimited number of times per year** ([MrGuider](https://www.mrguider.org/cheats/bitlife-life-simulator-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)). Sourcing on life stages is the weakest part of this research. Confirmed age gates are scattered: Mind & Body activities open at **12** ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_%26_Body)), freelance gigs at **13 or 14** (sources conflict), part-time jobs at 14, the full part-time list at 16, and full careers at **18** ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-part-time-jobs-guide/); [Touch, Tap, Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-get-part-time-job-in-bitlife/)). Fertility options and exotic pets also open at 18, and adoption at 21. The game scores how you play: spamming Age with no actions earns the **Lazy** ribbon ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-ribbons-guide-how-to-get-all-the-ribbons/)).

## School gates careers through Smarts, Popularity and graduate school

Version 1.23 ("School Update") added **Grades** (seeded from Smarts) and **Popularity**, plus cheating on tests, bullying and student–teacher interactions ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-school-update-guide-version-1-23-grades-and-popularity-cheating-on-tests-school-bullying-student-teacher-interactions-and-college-life-explained/)). Popularity unlocks **cliques** such as Nerds, Skaters and Mean Girls, and they can reject you ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Education/Cliques)). Mean Girls reportedly needs about **80% Looks**, and joining a sports team is the easy route to popularity ([Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-into-the-mean-girls-clique-in-bitlife/)). You can pay for university with parental money, a student loan or a scholarship ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=22687)). Graduate programs then gate the professional careers: **law, medical, business, dental, pharmacy, nursing and vet school**. Business school leads toward CEO, law school toward lawyer and then judge, dental school to dentist ([AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/bitlife/strategy-guide/school-guide); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-law-school-medical-school-any-school/)).

**Conflict flag:** guides disagree on which majors qualify. One maps Chemistry to dental, medical, pharmacy and vet; Political Science and English to law; and Psychology to medicine ([Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-schools-guide/)). Others say law needs Criminal Justice and dentistry needs Biology ([Progameguides law](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-go-to-law-school-in-bitlife/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-law-school-medical-school-any-school/)). The differences probably reflect changes between versions. The advice that holds across guides is Smarts of **80–85+**, with Health over 80 for doctors, and guides call law school the hardest to get into ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-get-into-medical-school-in-bitlife)). Version 1.22 also added an intelligence-test mini-game ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-back-to-school-update-guide-version-1-22-intelligence-test-mini-game-retirement-plans-career-collecting-work-hours-and-stress-levels-explained/)). Private, behavior and boarding schools appear only on a low-quality site, and no source covered community college, dropping out, or a GED.

## Disease, death and inheritance turn one life into a dynasty

Health threats come from many directions. **Untreated COVID kills quickly, malaria is very likely fatal within a year, and lupus within about a year**. Other routes to death include accidents, murder, assault, substance abuse, peacetime military accidents, old age and the always-available **surrender** (suicide) option ([Touch Tap Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-die-in-bitlife/); [playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)). Treatment runs through the **Doctor** in Activities. STDs come from unprotected clubbing sex or hookups ([Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-achievements-guide/)). A **witch doctor** cure earns the Witchcraft achievement ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements)), and its death risk rises with each visit ([Touch Tap Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-die-in-bitlife/)). One guide says the witch doctor is reachable only while homeless, but that detail comes from DogLife, so treat it as unconfirmed for BitLife ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/doglife-bitlife-dogs-achievement-guide-how-to-unlock-every-achievement-in-the-game/)). **Rehab** cures addictions, and the casino can create a gambling addiction whether you win or lose ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-to-complete-the-love-isle-challenge-gambling-surgery-hook/)). Health achievements include Addicted (three addictions at once), Bubonic Plague, Foam at the Mouth (rabies), Sickly (10 diseases in one life) and Successful Rehab ([Progameguides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-achievements-guide/)). **Plastic surgery** is the fastest and most expensive route to 100% Looks, with Botox as the cheapest procedure. **Salon & Spa**, open from the teen years, offers hair, nails, tanning, waxing and massage ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-improve-looks/)). No source covered mental health or therapy.

At death a **tombstone** shows age, final net worth, achievements and one ribbon ([playbitlifegame.com](https://www.playbitlifegame.com/)). You can start fresh or continue as an eligible child, adopted children included. That option dates to about **v1.14** ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-14-faq-how-to-continue-as-your-child-how-to-repeat-a-bitlife-and-how-to-become-a-model/)). This is a key monetization gate: per the wiki, **Bitizens get unlimited generations, while non-Bitizens can continue only once** ([BitLife Wiki: Generations](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Generations)). A will in Activities can leave money to one child, a partner or charity, or split it. **Estate tax** applies in countries that have it in real life ([KiwiPoints](https://www.kiwipoints.com/how-to-avoid-estate-tax-in-bitlife/)). Heirs inherit houses and cars. If the heir is under 18, those assets are auctioned and the heir keeps the cash ([BitLife Wiki: Assets](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Assets)). As of 2022, startup businesses could not be passed to heirs ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/can-children-inherit-startup-businesses-in-bitlife/)). Whether that changed by 2026 is unknown.

## Careers run from part-time gigs to paid special-career packs

Ordinary work is free. In a job you can work harder, ask for a raise, request more hours or resign, and promotion odds rise with time in the role ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Careers/Job_activities)). Freelance gigs are one-off jobs where you set your own rate, while part-time jobs carry over year to year ([WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-full-guide-to-part-time-jobs-and-freelancing/)). Updates added **job interviews (v1.21)**, then retirement plans, career collecting, work hours and **stress (v1.22)** ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-21-guide-prison-life-riot-mini-game-family-heirlooms-job-interviews-and-more-explained/); [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-back-to-school-update-guide-version-1-22-intelligence-test-mini-game-retirement-plans-career-collecting-work-hours-and-stress-levels-explained/)). An **Office update** added co-worker and supervisor relationships and HR reports, with hundreds of new workplace and military scenarios ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-office-update-manage-relationships-with-co-workers-and-supervisors-report-colleagues-to-hr-and-more-new-features-detailed/)). The **Monk and Nun** careers arrived in v1.18.1 ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-18-1-guide-scenario-creator-new-countries-cities-and-vacations-and-monk-and-nun-careers-explained/)). A **Summer 2025 update** added five careers, among them plastic surgeon ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-become-a-plastic-surgeon-in-bitlife/)). A pension historically needed about **20 years in one job** plus a minimum age. You can now retire at any time, but waiting for the employer pension is still best ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Careers/Jobs); [bitlifie](https://bitlifie.com/bitlife-jobs-guide/)). Regulated careers need credentials, for example law school for Judge and a license for Pilot ([bitlifewiki.com](https://bitlifewiki.com/bitlife-career-requirements-list/)). Military deployments arrived in v1.20 ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-20-guide-military-deployments-dark-mode-power-user-time-machine-and-more-explained/)).

**Special Careers** sit under Occupation, and each has prerequisites, often talents built through childhood lessons ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-special-careers-in-bitlife/)). As of October 2024 the wiki listed **ten special-career job packs**: Actor, Astronaut, Athlete, Business, Mafia, Musician, Politician, Street Hustler, Model and Dealer ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Careers/Occupation)). Actors start with acting lessons, then audition for TV and film. Musicians need instrument or voice lessons, then join a band or go solo. Athletes rise from school sports through college to the pros. Astronauts need a science or engineering degree and high Smarts ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-get-special-careers-in-bitlife/)). **Politics** has four levels, each needing a self-funded campaign, starting from posts like the School Board. Prime Minister is open in parliamentary countries but not in the US ([The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/bitlife-how-to-get-special-careers/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-how-become-president/)). **Royalty** is not a career: you are born or marry into it in monarchies. Prima lists 17 (Belgium, Denmark, Japan, Jordan, Kuwait, Malaysia, Monaco, Morocco, Netherlands, Norway, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Spain, Sweden, Thailand, UAE, UK), and Gamer Tweak's list adds Barbados ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/what-countries-have-royalty-in-bitlife-answered); [Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/how-to-become-royalty-in-bitlife/)). The September 2020 Royalty update added a **Respect** meter ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Royalty)). Qatar and the UAE use emir/emira as the top rank, and some countries bar execution even for the monarch ([KiwiPoints](https://www.kiwipoints.com/all-countries-with-royalty-in-bitlife/)).

**Fame** arrived in April 2019 ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Stats/Fame)). It comes through acting, music, sports and modeling, or through **social media**. Verification thresholds conflict across sources: 100k followers plus 25 fame, 100k on one platform, or about 250k. Reaching "Social Media Star" at about **300k–350k** followers unlocks the Fame tab ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-how-to-become-an-influencer/); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/bitlife/news/how-to-be-famous-in-bitlife)). These figures are about four years old.

## Money flows through businesses, markets, property and the casino

The **Business** job pack covers **25 industries**. You can found a company or take one over as CEO through Special Careers > Business, create products, and manage employee pay and morale ([GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/news?id=46583); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-run-a-successful-business)). An IPO option shows up only on an unreliable page. The **investment update** (around December 2022) and the Stock Market update added stocks, crypto, bonds, penny stocks and funds, a hireable and fireable financial advisor, and a liquidate-everything button for crashes ([AppleVis](https://applevis.com/forum/ios-ipados-gaming/bitlife-investment-update); [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/news?id=61303)). You can buy houses with cash or a mortgage. Flipping means using Renovate before selling ([GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/bitlife-how-to-buy-a-house/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-flip-houses-in-bitlife-and-sell-them-guide/)). Renting to tenants needs the paid **Landlord Expansion Pack**, announced 21 February and released **9 March 2023** ([BitLife on X](https://twitter.com/BitLifeApp/status/1633922721209020420?lang=en); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-make-money-as-a-landlord-in-bitlife)). Jewelry loses value every year ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Assets)). **Heirlooms** come from a daily attic mini-game added in v1.21, count toward net worth, and pass to the heir ([BitLife Zendesk](https://bitlife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038370612-What-are-Heirlooms)).

The free **casino** offers blackjack and horse-race betting. The wiki says only blackjack is free, so horse racing's status is unclear ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-life-simulator-highroller-ribbon/); [BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Casino)). The paid **Casino Expansion Pack** adds 8 games, for **9 in total**, including Keno, 14-slot Roulette, slots and High-Low, plus doubling down and splitting in blackjack. Official X teased it around November 2024, and it reportedly shipped **18 November 2024** ([BitLife on X](https://x.com/BitLifeApp/status/1852425492353180064?lang=en); [Pop Flop](https://x.com/ThePopFlop/status/1858614218913702021)). One fan blog still calls it "upcoming", which is stale. The **lottery** jackpot earns the Jackpot achievement ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-achievements-guide/)). A free **cybersecurity hacking** feature was reportedly added by 2025, per an unofficial aggregator ([Ezechax](https://ezechax.club/bitlife-update/)).

## Crime escalates from shoplifting to the Mafia and death row

Activities > Crimes offers shoplifting, porch piracy, pickpocketing, burglary, grand theft auto, drug trafficking, murder, **contract killing** (hiring a hitman), and from v1.31 **embezzlement** from your job ([BitLife Wiki: Mafia](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia); [Happy Gamer](https://happygamer.com/bitlife-for-android-follows-prison-update-by-adding-more-criminal-activities-in-update-1-22-1-56640/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-31-update-guide-weekly-challenges-relationship-counselling-embezzlement-and-more/)). **Bank robbery** (18+) depends on your choice of disguise, weapon and getaway vehicle ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-rob-a-bank-in-bitlife/)). **Train robbery** works only when the real train runs, and you can succeed only once ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-train-robbery-guide-how-to-rob-a-train-and-get-away-with-it/)). The **Mafia Update (v1.46, December 2020)** added six families: Irish Mob, Latin Mafia, Mafia, Russian Mafia, Triad and Yakuza. Ranks run from Associate to Godfather, and you can extort businesses for up to **$25,000** each time ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia_Update); [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-mafia-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-bitlife-version-1-46-aka-the-mafia-update/); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/bitlife/news/how-to-join-the-mafia-in-bitlife)).

The **Prison update (v1.21)** added prison jobs, bribing guards, gangs, and a **riot mini-game**: you chain inmates, guards appear at 7, and 10 or more wins. The same update made caught serial killers almost certain to get the death penalty ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-prison-update-guide-version-1-21-prison-jobs-prison-riot-mini-game-bribing-guards-joining-gangs-explained/)). Gangs are more common in higher-security prisons, and pricier lawyers improve appeal odds ([WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-complete-updated-prison-and-jail-guide/)). The **escape** mini-game is a grid maze where the guard moves twice for each of your moves, and getting caught adds years. Grid sizes conflict: one source says 4x4 at minimum security up to 8x8 at maximum, another says 3x4 is the smallest, and a third counts 14 layouts ([Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/bitlife-escape-prison/); [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/games/how-to-escape-prison-in-bitlife-211525/); [iTechHacks](https://itechhacks.com/how-to-escape-prison-in-bitlife/)). If you flee to another country to escape justice through Activities > Emigrate, you earn "Flee the Country" ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-emigrate-to-escape-justice-in-bitlife-flee-the-country-achievement/)). No source documented sentence lengths, trials or parole.

## Relationships, children and pets fill the social tabs

Every relative has a **relationship bar**. Spending time, conversation, compliments and gifts raise it. Insults, assault and repeated money requests lower it, and a low bar makes NPCs more likely to argue with or attack you ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/bitlife-improve-relationships-how/)). Stepfamilies arrived in v1.24, along with arranged marriage and free healthcare ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-stepfamily-update-guide-version-1-24-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-stepfamily-update/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-stepfamily-update-stepparents-stepsiblings-stepchildren-arranged-marriage-free-healthcare-and-more/)). Multiple friends and enemies arrived in v1.28 ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-1-28-update-guide-a-complete-look-at-bitlife-version-1-28-aka-the-friends-update/)), and relationship counselling in v1.31.

Romance runs through Activities > Love. The **dating app** filters by age and net worth, and dates cost about **$100**; hookups are the other route ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-marry-someone-rich-in-bitlife/)). The Pride update added a gay dating app. You can have **one lover at a time**, but either partner can cheat ([BitLife Wiki: Hook-Up](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Hook-Up)). To marry, buy a ring, propose, then plan a wedding with tiered venues. Fiancés demand a wedding after about three years ([BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Relationships/Spouses)). A wealthy partner may demand a **prenup**. Without one, divorce splits assets 50/50, and a prenup protects only assets from before the marriage ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/what-does-a-prenup-do-in-bitlife-answered)). **Arranged marriage** happens in certain countries (lists conflict: Afghanistan, India, Iran, Japan, with some adding China, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia), and refusing it ruins your relationship with both parents ([KiwiPoints](https://www.kiwipoints.com/how-to-get-an-arranged-marriage-in-bitlife/); [Distractify](https://www.distractify.com/p/how-to-get-arranged-marriage-bitlife)). No source confirmed polygamy, dowry, custody or child support.

From 18, **Activities > Fertility** offers IVF, Surrogate, Donate Sperm and Vasectomy. Surrogacy costs about **$25k to $100k+** and is legal only where it is in real life ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/where-is-surrogacy-legal-in-bitlife/)). IVF can produce twins or triplets ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bitlife-twins-triplets-how-ivf-artificial-insemination-fertility/)). **Adoption** needs age 21+, a job and a home ([BitLife Wiki: Fertility](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Fertility)). Pets come from shelters and breeders. **Exotic pets, horses and llamas are 18+**: the Exotic Pet Dealer sells one per year, and you can also rescue animals in random encounters. Horses need an "equestrian" property costing about $5–15M, unless the horse is a rescued unicorn ([BitLife Wiki: Exotic pet](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Exotic_pet); [WriterParty](https://writerparty.com/party/bitlife-complete-pets-guide-list-of-all-pets-and-how-to-get-and-use-them/)). Exotic pets are reportedly a Bitizen perk ([Hwb](https://hwb.gov.wales/keeping-safe-online/in-the-know/bitlife)). The **Zoo** pack (v3.14.1, May or June 2024 depending on the source) adds small, medium and large zoos ([BitLife Wiki: Zoo](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Zoo); [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/when-is-the-zookeeper-expansion-pack-update-coming-to-bitlife/)).

The rest of the **Activities menu** includes Mind & Body (gym, library, meditation, memory test), Emigrate (approval depends on criminal record and money, with illegal emigration as a fallback), Lawsuit (appears only with a valid grievance; a $1M+ win earns "Rich Justice"; once reported missing on Android), and Licenses. A driver's license means a traffic-sign test (free or $100), a boating license means one question ($90, $255 or $405 across sources), and a pilot license needs about 40 hours of flight school at $165–$335 per hour ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/how-to-emigrate-in-bitlife/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-how-to-file-a-lawsuit-against-and-how-they-work/); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-obtain-every-license-in-bitlife)). **Identity**, added in v1.38, handles gender identity, orientation and legal name changes (18+, a few hundred dollars). Gender changes are allowed from age 4–5 or at any age depending on the source, and sexuality changes from 16 ([BitLife Wiki: Identity](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Identity); [Touch, Tap, Play](https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-legally-change-your-name-in-bitlife/)). Vacations, Nightlife, Shopping and Surrender to police appear in guide category lists ([MrGuider](https://www.mrguider.org/cheats/bitlife-life-simulator-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)), but no source detailed their mechanics.

## Premium tiers stack, and every price figure conflicts

The Marketplace splits into four tiers. **Essentials** are Bitizenship, God Mode and a bundle of the two. **Boss Mode** covers job packs. **Expansion packs and Modes** are sold one by one. **Consumables** are Time Machine uses. The distinction that matters most comes from official sources. The Help Center says "Boss Mode is a one-time purchase that unlocks **all current and future Job Packs** including Mafia, Pro Athlete, Musician, Actor, and more" ([BitLife Zendesk](https://bitlife.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5165069804948-What-is-Boss-Mode)). BitLife's X account confirms that "**Investor and Landlord are expansion packs**", so Boss Mode does not include them ([BitLife on X](https://x.com/BitLifeApp/status/1637882951894482945?lang=en)).

**Bitizenship** is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads and adds "VIP" content: unlimited generations, exotic pets, special character interactions ([Hwb](https://hwb.gov.wales/keeping-safe-online/in-the-know/bitlife)), plus dark mode, a pet store and breeders, boss and teacher interactions, hitmen, bands, prison gangs and mafia events ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/bitlife/bitlife-bitizen/)). A cheaper ad-remover-only purchase also exists. **God Mode** arrived in v1.33. It edits anyone's name, appearance and stats, including hidden ones like Karma, Fertility and Athleticism, and can set royal status ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bitlife-god-mode-update-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-god-mode-and-other-new-features-in-bitlife-version-1-33/); [BitLife Wiki](https://bitlife-life-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/God_Mode)). It cannot change birthday, siblings or conception method, and appearance edits apply only at ages 21–44 ([Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/god-mode-bitlife/)). Players report that you can keep editing NPCs during play but can edit yourself only at creation ([AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/110668)). The **Time Machine** arrived in v1.20 and lets you rewind to an earlier age, even after death. It is sold per use, with an "Unlimited Time Machine" SKU in some stores ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/bitlife-version-1-20-guide-military-deployments-dark-mode-power-user-time-machine-and-more-explained/); [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/culture/top-10-best-bitlife-expansions-and-job-packs-worth-buying/)).

## Conclusion

BitLife's history splits in two. From 2018 to 2022, large free version updates (prison, school, royalty, Mafia) built a broad sandbox. From 2023, paid expansion packs and one-time "Modes" took over as the main way new systems arrive. Boss Mode's "all future job packs" promise does not cover any of them, which explains both the rising cost of owning everything and the community friction. The 2026 changes (ending Seasons, merging the language apps, three large paid releases in eleven months) look like a product being consolidated around paid content, with challenges and sweepstakes carrying the free side. If the cadence holds, another paid Mode is plausible in early 2027, but nothing confirms one.

The evidence is weakest where the game is most popular. Ribbon and achievement totals, Bitizenship and Boss Mode prices, verification thresholds and graduate-school requirements all vary across guides, partly because the game changes and partly because no official reference exists. Anyone relying on these numbers, especially prices and ribbon or achievement totals, should check them in the current build or on the regional store page. Treat the fan-guide figures here as historical baselines, not live values.
